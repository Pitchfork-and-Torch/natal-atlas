      Questions

      Does birth data leave this device?
      No. Cast, vault, and overlays run in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
      Which zodiac and houses?
      Tropical apparent geocentric. Porphyry by default, or whole sign or equal. Solar chart if the birth time is unknown.
      Can two charts share a wheel?
      Yes. Save a chart in the Vault and set it as the outer ring. Rings are mutually exclusive: transits, progressed, or vault.
      How do I wind time?
      After a cast, the chronograph under the wheel winds Hour, Day, Month, Year, or Decade. Arrow keys step. Space plays. Nothing is uploaded.
